DESIGNATION OF OPEN GAME CONTENT

The game-rule material in the following places is Open Game Content, as defined in Section 1(d) of
the licence, and may be used under the terms of the licence:

  - the game mechanics (formulas and procedures) that the scripts in src/rules/ implement.
